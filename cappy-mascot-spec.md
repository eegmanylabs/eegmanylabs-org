# Cappy mascot specification

## Canonical identity

Cappy is a neutral, non-ethnic mascot. Use the supplied reference as the fixed identity model: a round white face with two simple vertical black oval eyes and one simple curved black smile, no nose, no hair, no skin tone, no facial hair, and no glasses. The body uses a white lab coat with clean black outlines, black trousers, grey shoes, and simple mitten-like hands. Cappy wears the same rounded EEG electrode cap with a curved striped ponytail. The cap has black-outlined electrode circles and simple lead lines. The silhouette, facial proportions, black outline weight, and friendly expression must remain recognisably consistent in every image.

## Revised colour system for illustrations

Use warm charcoal outlines and text rather than navy. Keep white or warm-white backgrounds. Use coral red or terracotta red for the EEG cap, selected clothing details, and small scientific accents. Use warm grey for neutral hardware or objects. Avoid blue and green. Do not add ethnicity, gender presentation, hair, skin colour, or distinctive facial characteristics.

## Asset scope

Regenerate the illustrative, non-logo assets as a coherent Cappy set: homepage Replications, Core Team, Members, Publications, Related Projects, and Partners cards; related-project Asymmetry, Automation, Prediction Markets, Resting State, and ElectroTime art. Do not modify headshots, partner logos, funder marks, paper thumbnails, or scientific figure images.

## ElectroTime

Use a simple square illustration of canonical Cappy holding a single plain analogue clock. No glasses, ECG monitor, EEG traces, cables, stopwatch labels, text, or additional equipment. The clock alone should communicate the subject.
