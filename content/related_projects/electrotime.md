ElectroTime is an open-source, low-cost (under $80) device that measures the real delay between your trigger and the moment a stimulus actually reaches the participant — visual or auditory, on any M/EEG setup. Born out of #EEGManyLabs, it helps labs detect and correct the timing variations that can otherwise distort results and break replication.
