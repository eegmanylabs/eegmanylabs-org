# #EEGManyLabs Hub: requirements checklist

This checklist restates every distinct, testable requirement of the master build prompt for the #EEGManyLabs Hub, one table per section of that prompt. Each row carries a stable identifier, the delivery phase from section 19 that owns it, and a status. The status vocabulary is fixed: `planned` means the work has not started, `in progress` means work is under way, `implemented` means the feature exists in the codebase, `tested` means it is implemented and covered by a recorded passing test, and `blocked` means the item cannot be completed until a named dependency outside the codebase is supplied. Where a requirement spans several phases, the Phase column names the phase in which it is first delivered and the Notes column says so.

Once an item reaches `implemented` or `tested`, Evidence points at the test, source file or document that demonstrates it, so that a reader can verify each claim independently. Paths that begin with `shared/`, `web/`, `functions/`, `tools/` or `tests/`, and the files `firestore.rules` and `firestore.indexes.json`, are relative to `portal/`. Other paths are relative to the repository root. An item is marked `tested` only where an automated test, or a check recorded in `docs/TEST_REPORT.md`, exercises the code. The automated tests cited for phases 1 to 3 passed in the independent runs of the full suite recorded in sections 4 and 5 of that report. Those cited for phases 4 and 5 passed in the independent run recorded in section 6. Those cited for phases 6 and 7 passed in the independent run recorded in section 7. Those cited for phases 8 and 9, including the fixes that followed their review, passed in the independent run recorded in section 8, in which the browser tests run in three batches with freshly started emulators. Those cited for phase 10, which also closed the open gaps of phases 2 to 5, passed in the runs reported by the engineers who built and reviewed it, each part of `npm test` in a run of its own: one uninterrupted run of the whole suite after the final fixes, and its record in `docs/TEST_REPORT.md`, are still to come. All of that testing ran on the Firebase emulators with synthetic fixtures or the demonstration world, so `tested` never means verified in a deployed environment. In phases 6 to 9 the Google Workspace, the research data servers, OSF, the website's GitHub repository and the mail sender are replaced by in-memory fakes, and the real adapters have never been executed. An item that depends on what Google or a real server does stays `blocked` even where its logic is tested against a fake, and its note says so; an item that the portal decides alone, such as a planner, a workflow or a rule, is marked `tested` against the fake. An item that is only partly met stays `in progress`, and its note says what is missing. Statuses were last reviewed on 13 November 2026, after phase 10 and the fixes of its review. Phase 10 prepares deployment without performing it, so every item that needs a staging project, credentials, a deployment or a decision of the programme stays `blocked` and its note names what it needs; `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` is the runbook that unblocks the deployment items. The seven mandatory sources are referred to by letter and title only: A historical replication coordination workbook, B naming conventions, C community vocabulary, D list of studies, E prediction-market hypotheses, F recruitment and contact workbook, and G membership and contributor workbook. Source locations and identifiers are held in the private source registry and are deliberately absent from this public file.
